Economic Growth & Renewable Energy

This project explored the relationship between economic growth, renewable energy adoption and CO₂ emissions. It asked whether countries investing heavily in renewable energy are transitioning away from fossil fuels quickly enough.

The analysis compares renewable energy use and emissions to help frame questions about energy transition, investment and sustainability.

Tools and focus: SQL · Data analysis · Renewable energy · CO₂ emissions
