My story

Connecting business understanding with data.

I am Miracle Ozioma Nwokeocha, a data analyst and business administration professional with an interest in turning information into useful business insight. I enjoy working through complex problems, finding patterns and communicating findings in a way that supports action.

My experience and learning have exposed me to healthcare data, renewable energy research, financial analysis and business operations. These experiences have strengthened my ability to approach unfamiliar questions with curiosity, structure and attention to detail.

I believe effective analysis combines technical skills with context: understanding the question, checking the quality of the data, interpreting the results and presenting them clearly.
