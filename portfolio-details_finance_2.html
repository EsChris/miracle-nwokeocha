Global Social Media Users & Behaviour

This project explored the use of social media platforms and the behaviour of users around the world. It considered time spent on social platforms, the range of platforms people use and differences in usage patterns across age groups.

The presentation used research and descriptive analysis to explain how social media has become part of everyday digital communication.
